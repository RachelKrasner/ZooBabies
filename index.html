How this works: a TinyFish Search finds fresh zoo birth news, Fetch pulls each full article, and a TinyFish Agent reads the page and fills in the birth card (species, name, sex, birth date, parents, conservation status). A TinyFish Monitor re-runs the search every morning at 8am London time so new babies keep showing up. Conservation status is the IUCN Red List category.
